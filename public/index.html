    How to use this calculator

    Enter a sum the way you would write it, then press =. The top line of the display shows what you typed; the large line shows the answer as you go, so you can check it before you finish.

    After an answer, press an operator such as + to keep going from that result, or a number to start a new sum. Press ⌫ to remove the last thing you typed and AC to start again.

    Order of operations

    The calculator follows the standard order: brackets first, then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. So 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20. Use brackets when you want a different order: (2 + 3) × 4 is 20.

    Scientific mode

    Switch to Scientific for trigonometry, logarithms, powers, roots and factorials. Trig functions use degrees unless you switch the angle unit to radians. A number written next to a bracket or π multiplies it, so 2π is the same as 2 × π.

    Questions

    How does the percent key work?
    On its own, 25% is 0.25. After a plus or minus it works like a shop calculator: 80 + 10% adds 10% of 80 and gives 88, and 80 − 25% gives 60.
    Where is my tape saved?
    Only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent anywhere. Clear it any time with Clear tape.
    Why does the answer say "Can't divide by zero"?
    Dividing by zero has no answer. Change the number after the ÷ and press = again.
    How precise are the answers?
    Answers are rounded to 12 significant digits, which hides the tiny rounding errors computers make with decimals, such as 0.1 + 0.2.
