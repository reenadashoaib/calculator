How the payment is worked out

This is a standard fixed-rate loan, paid monthly. The payment is

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

where P is the amount borrowed, r is the yearly rate divided by 12, and n is the number of months. Early payments are mostly interest; as the balance falls, more of each payment goes to the loan itself.

Ways to pay less interest

  • Shorter term. Try 15 years instead of 30: the monthly payment goes up, but total interest usually falls by more than half.
  • Lower rate. Even half a percentage point makes a large difference over a long loan.
  • Bigger deposit. Borrowing less lowers both the payment and the interest.

This calculator does not include taxes, insurance or fees, which can add to a real monthly payment. Check the final figures with your lender.
