How age is counted

Age goes up by one year on each birthday. The months count full calendar months since the last birthday, and the days count what is left over. Someone born on 15 June 1990 is 36 years, 4 months and 10 days old on 25 October 2026.

Change the "Age on" date to find how old someone was, or will be, on any day: a wedding, a school start date, or a retirement date.

Leap-year birthdays

If you were born on 29 February, this calculator counts your birthday as 28 February in years without a 29th. Some countries use 1 March for legal purposes instead.

Questions

Does it account for leap years?
Yes. Total days uses the real calendar, including every 29 February between the two dates.
Is my date of birth stored?
No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent.
