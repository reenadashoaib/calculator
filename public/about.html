About Calcdesk

Free calculators for everyday sums, money, health and dates.

Calcdesk is a set of quick, free calculators that work on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

Every calculation runs in your own browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us. The calculator tape is saved only on your device so you can come back to it.

How we keep it free

The site shows advertising. Ads are always labelled "Advertisement" and kept apart from the calculators so they never get in the way of a result.

Accuracy

We check each calculator against worked examples, and each page explains the formula it uses. Results are for general information; for loans, health and legal dates, confirm important figures with a professional.
