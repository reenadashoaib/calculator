What the numbers mean

BMICategory for adults
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 to 24.9Healthy weight
25 to 29.9Overweight
30 or moreObesity

These are the World Health Organization ranges for adults aged 18 and over. Children and teenagers are measured against growth charts for their age and sex instead.

The formula

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared: BMI = kg ÷ m². Someone 1.75 m tall who weighs 70 kg has a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9. In imperial units the calculator converts pounds and inches to metric first.

Limits of BMI

BMI does not tell muscle from fat, so athletes can score as overweight. It also says nothing about where fat is carried, and the same number can mean different health risks for different ethnic groups and ages. Treat it as a starting point, and talk to a doctor about what is healthy for you.
