The formulas

  • Percent of a number: part = percent ÷ 100 × number. 15% of 240 is 0.15 × 240 = 36.
  • Percent of a total: percent = part ÷ total × 100. 36 out of 240 is 36 ÷ 240 × 100 = 15%.
  • Percent change: change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Going from 80 to 92 is (92 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 15% more.

Common uses

Discounts

A 30% discount on a price of 60 takes off 30% of 60, which is 18, so you pay 42. Quick check: paying 70% of 60 gives the same 42.

Pay rises and price changes

Use percent change to compare two values over time. A salary that goes from 42,000 to 45,150 has risen by 7.5%.

Test scores

Use percent of a total to turn a score into a percentage. 43 correct out of 50 questions is 86%.

Questions

Is a percent increase followed by the same percent decrease back to the start?
No. Add 10% to 100 and you get 110; take 10% off 110 and you get 99. The second percentage is of a bigger number.
What is the difference between percent and percentage points?
If an interest rate moves from 4% to 5%, it rose by 1 percentage point, but by 25 percent of its old value.
