How much to tip

Customs vary a lot by country. In the United States, 15% to 20% is usual at sit-down restaurants. In the UK and much of Europe, 10% is common, and some bills already include a service charge, so check before adding more. In Japan, tipping is not expected.

Tipping in your head

Start with 10%: move the decimal point one place left, so 10% of 86.40 is 8.64. Double it for 20% (17.28). For 15%, add half of the 10% figure to itself: 8.64 + 4.32 = 12.96.

Questions

Should the tip be on the total before or after tax?
Many people tip on the amount before tax. Enter the pre-tax amount as the bill if you prefer that.
Why does the split not add up exactly?
Per-person amounts are rounded to two decimal places, so they can differ from the total by a cent.
